**When is the best time of year to get married in Paris?**

**Spring (April–June)** — Our most popular season. Mild temperatures (15–22°C), cherry blossoms in April, blooming gardens, long days. Book at least 12–18 months ahead.

**Summer (July–August)** — Warm and sunny with beautiful long evenings. Note that many Parisian vendors take their annual holiday in August, which can limit supplier availability. July is excellent; August weddings require careful advance planning.

**Autumn (September–October)** — A favourite among photographers for the golden light and rich foliage colours. Cooler evenings (bring a wrap for outdoor ceremonies), fewer tourists in the city. Highly recommended.

**Winter (November–March)** — Intimate, magical, and significantly more affordable for venues and suppliers. Paris during the holidays is particularly romantic. Indoor venues with fireplaces or candles make for stunning atmospheres. Lower demand means more flexibility on dates.

**Peak demand months:** May, June, and September. For these months, venues often book 12–24 months in advance.

**Legal considerations on timing:** French law requires a minimum of 10 days between publication of banns and the civil ceremony. For foreign nationals, additional paperwork can add weeks to the timeline. We strongly advise beginning the administrative process at least 6 months before the desired date.
