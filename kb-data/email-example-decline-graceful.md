Subject: Re: Your Paris Wedding Inquiry

Dear Anna and Mark,

Thank you so much for reaching out and for sharing your vision — a Paris elopement with just your two closest friends sounds utterly romantic, and your instinct to choose this city is a beautiful one.

We want to be honest with you: our services are best suited to celebrations where we can offer the full scope of planning support, and for a very intimate gathering, the value we would add may not justify the investment in our fees.

However, we would not leave you without a helpful steer. For intimate elopements and micro-weddings in Paris, we would suggest exploring:
- A specialist elopement photographer who often packages ceremony coordination alongside photography
- The Paris mairie system for civil ceremonies, which is more accessible than many people realise
- The Marché des Fleurs (Île de la Cité) if you are dreaming of a spontaneous bouquet

We genuinely wish you the most magical day — Paris will not disappoint.

With warmth,
