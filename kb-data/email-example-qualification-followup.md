Subject: A Few Questions to Help Us Plan Your Day

Dear James and Clara,

Thank you so much for your message — congratulations on your engagement! A Paris wedding in the autumn sounds absolutely perfect.

To make sure we can give you the most relevant picture of what we can offer, it would help enormously to know a little more about your vision:

1. **Date flexibility** — Do you have a fixed date in mind, or are you open to a window of dates? (This can sometimes open up wonderful venue options.)
2. **Guest count** — Are you imagining an intimate gathering or a larger celebration?
3. **Venue type** — A historic château, a Haussmannian private mansion, a garden in the Bois de Boulogne, or perhaps a rooftop with a view of the Eiffel Tower?
4. **Services** — Are you looking for full planning and coordination, or do you already have some elements in place and need support with specific aspects?
5. **Budget range** — Even a rough sense helps us tailor our proposal and recommend the right venues and suppliers.

There are no wrong answers at all — we simply want to make sure our first conversation is as useful as possible for you.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Warmly,
Marie
Dream Paris Wedding
