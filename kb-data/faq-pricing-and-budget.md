**What does a Paris wedding cost?**

A Paris or Île-de-France destination wedding typically ranges from €25,000 to €150,000+ depending on guest count, venue, season, and the level of customisation desired.

**Venue hire alone** for a premium château or private mansion in the Paris region ranges from €5,000 to €30,000+ per day.

**Catering** for a seated dinner typically runs €120–€250 per person for high-quality Parisian caterers, inclusive of service and rental.

**Dream Paris Wedding planning fees** are structured as follows:
- Full planning: From €8,000 (based on scope and guest count)
- Partial planning: From €3,500
- Day-of coordination only: From €2,000

**What is the minimum budget we work with?**
We typically work with weddings with an overall budget of €30,000 or above. Below that threshold, we are unfortunately unable to deliver the quality of experience our clients deserve.

**Are there payment plans?**
Yes. A deposit of 30% is required upon signing the contract. The balance is typically split across two further milestones before the wedding date.

**Do prices include VAT?**
All our fees are quoted excluding French VAT (TVA) at 20%. We will clearly indicate this on every proposal and invoice.
