**What services does Dream Paris Wedding offer?**

Dream Paris Wedding offers a full range of destination wedding services for couples planning to marry in Paris and the surrounding Île-de-France region:

**Full Planning & Coordination**
End-to-end service from the initial vision session through to the wedding day itself. We handle venue sourcing, supplier selection and management, legal and administrative requirements, timeline creation, and on-the-day coordination.

**Partial Planning (À la Carte)**
For couples who have already begun planning and need support with specific elements — perhaps venue sourcing only, supplier recommendations, or day-of coordination.

**Photography**
We work with an exclusive network of Paris-based photographers whose aesthetic aligns with our brand: timeless, editorial, and deeply personal. We can match couples with the right photographer for their vision and style.

**Floral Design**
Through our partner florists, we offer bespoke floral concepts from intimate ceremony arrangements to full venue transformation.

**Administrative Support**
Navigating French marriage law as a foreign couple can be complex. We guide clients through the paperwork, residency requirements, and local administrative steps.

**What we do not offer:**
We do not provide catering services directly, but we work with an outstanding network of Parisian caterers and private chefs we trust completely.
