**When is the team available for discovery calls?**

We schedule discovery calls on **Mondays and Wednesdays** to ensure we give each couple our full, unhurried attention.

Call slots are typically available:
- 10:00–12:00 CET (morning sessions)
- 14:00–17:00 CET (afternoon sessions)

For clients in significantly different time zones (USA West Coast, Australia, Southeast Asia), we can occasionally arrange early morning CET slots on the same days — please mention your time zone when requesting a call.

**How long is a discovery call?**

30 minutes. This is enough time to understand your vision, answer your initial questions, and give you a genuine sense of how we work. There is no obligation, and no sales pressure.

**Can I email instead of calling?**

Absolutely. Many clients prefer to exchange a few detailed emails before committing to a call. We respond to all emails within one business day.

**What happens after the call?**

If there is a good fit on both sides, we prepare a tailored proposal within 5 business days. The proposal outlines the scope of work, our fees, and a proposed timeline. There is no obligation to proceed.

**Emergency or urgent matters for existing clients:**

Existing clients have a dedicated WhatsApp line for urgent matters. This is shared in your Welcome Pack after signing.
