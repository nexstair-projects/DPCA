## The Dream Paris Wedding Journey

### Phase 1 — Discovery (Months 12–18 before wedding)
- Initial inquiry received and responded to within 24 hours
- Discovery call scheduled (30 min, complimentary)
- Tailored proposal sent within 5 business days of call
- Contract signed and 30% deposit received to confirm the booking

### Phase 2 — Foundation (Months 9–12)
- Kick-off planning session (video call, 90 min)
- Venue sourcing and shortlisting (if not already chosen)
- Venue site visit coordinated
- Key supplier briefings: photographer, caterer, florist
- Legal and administrative requirements reviewed; paperwork checklist provided

### Phase 3 — Build (Months 6–9)
- Venue contract signed
- Core suppliers confirmed and contracted
- Ceremony and reception timeline drafted
- Guest accommodation recommendations shared
- Invitations and stationery guidance (if requested)

### Phase 4 — Detail (Months 3–6)
- Final guest count confirmed with venue and caterer
- Menu tasting completed
- Floral brief finalised
- Day-of timeline refined
- Transport and logistics coordinated
- Final payment (2nd instalment) due

### Phase 5 — Final Preparation (Months 1–3)
- Final venue walkthrough (in person or virtual)
- Detailed run-of-day document prepared and shared with all suppliers
- Rehearsal coordinated (if applicable)
- Emergency and contingency plan confirmed
- Final balance payment due 30 days before wedding date

### Phase 6 — Wedding Day
- Lead coordinator present from setup to end of reception
- All supplier arrivals managed
- Couple cared for, relaxed, and fully present

### Phase 7 — After the Wedding
- Post-wedding debrief call (within 2 weeks)
- Review and testimonial request
- Gallery delivery coordination (photography)
- Thank-you note sent
