title: "FAQ: Travel and Logistics for Destination Weddings"
category: "faq"
---

**Do you work with couples from outside France?**

Yes — the vast majority of our clients are international couples, primarily from the UK, USA, Australia, UAE, and Southeast Asia. We are experienced at managing all communication, planning, and legal coordination remotely and in English.

**How many site visits do we need to make before the wedding?**

We typically recommend one venue scouting trip (2–3 days in Paris) approximately 9–12 months before the wedding, and a final preparation visit 1–2 months beforehand. Many couples also come for a food tasting. We can consolidate visits to minimise travel.

**Can everything be managed remotely?**

Yes. We handle all supplier communication, contract review, and coordination on your behalf. We use video calls for all key planning meetings and provide detailed written summaries of every decision.

**Can you help arrange accommodation for our guests?**

We can recommend and coordinate room blocks at hotels near the venue, ranging from boutique Parisian hotels to château accommodation on-site. Guest accommodation is not included in our standard planning fee but we manage the coordination as part of our service.

**What about transport on the day?**

We coordinate chauffeur-driven transfers (cars, minibuses, or vintage Citroën DS for the couple) from accommodation to ceremony and reception. This is arranged through our trusted transport partners.

**Do you have English-speaking suppliers?**

Yes. Our entire preferred supplier network operates comfortably in English.
