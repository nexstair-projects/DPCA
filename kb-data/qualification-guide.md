Use this guide to assess whether a new inquiry is a strong fit for Dream Paris Wedding. Strong leads should be prioritised and moved quickly to a discovery call.

## Lead Scoring Signals

### Strong Fit Indicators
- Budget range explicitly stated above €30,000, or implied by guest count and venue aspirations
- Date is 6+ months away (sufficient planning time)
- Couple has a clear vision (venue type, season, style)
- Guest count between 20–150 (our sweet spot)
- Referral from a past client or trusted partner
- Previous contact with our brand (Instagram, website, etc.)

### Requires Careful Handling
- Budget not yet stated — ask gently with context ("to make sure we tailor the right proposal for you")
- Date is less than 6 months away — be honest about timeline constraints, but explore if it is viable
- Very large guest count (200+) — we can accommodate, but clarify early as this significantly impacts venue options and fees
- Very small guest count (fewer than 15) — be warm but explain minimum scope for our full service

### Likely Not a Fit
- Budget clearly below €20,000 once costs are understood
- Couple wants a venue we cannot support (e.g. highly religious venue with specific requirements outside our network)
- Seeking a civil ceremony only with no celebration (registry office only)

## Qualification Questions to Ask
1. What date or period are you considering?
2. How many guests are you envisioning?
3. Do you have a venue in mind, or would you like our guidance?
4. What is your approximate overall budget for the wedding?
5. What matters most to you — is it the photography, the food, the flowers, or the overall experience?

## After the Call
- If strong fit: send our Welcome Pack and schedule a follow-up within 5 business days
- If potential fit: keep warm, offer to answer specific questions, revisit in 2–4 weeks
- If not a fit: respond warmly, decline gracefully, and offer any free guidance you can
